NEED 2
English presents more of a challenge for Amelia, however, her single word reading skills, reading fluency, and comprehension all fall within the average range suggesting that she has the foundational skills necessary for continued growth in this area.
Outcomes
E3: Amelia will be meeting age related expectations in literacy (specifically spelling, punctuation and grammar) and numeracy as evidenced by appropriate assessments and ongoing teacher evaluations.

SMART Revision: By [end of academic year/specific date], Amelia will improve her spelling accuracy from [baseline X%] to [target Y%] on age-appropriate curriculum spelling tests, demonstrate correct use of punctuation and grammar in 7 out of 10 writing samples as measured by [specific rubric], and achieve a standardized score of [target score] on the [named numeracy assessment e.g., GL Assessment Mathematics Progress Test], representing progress appropriate to her learning profile with Dyslexia. Progress will be reviewed termly through specified assessments and teacher work sampling.

Note: This outcome is weak and vague. It lacks specificity in several ways: (1) "Age-related expectations" is undefined and difficult to measure; (2) It combines multiple distinct skills (spelling, punctuation, grammar, numeracy) without separate baselines or targets; (3) "Appropriate assessments" is not specified; (4) There is no timeframe or baseline for measuring progress; (5) It does not acknowledge Amelia's Dyslexia and the need for differentiated expectations. This outcome should be redesigned to include: specific baseline scores, targeted improvement goals for each skill area separately, named standardized assessments, clear timeframes, and realistic expectations given Amelia's learning profile.

NEED 3
Amelia's specific learning difficulties, including Dyslexia, pose challenges to her progress especially in the subject of English. She experiences anxiety around understanding and completing learning tasks and exhibits noticeable fatigue during extended writing tasks.
Outcomes
E4: Amelia will utilise at least two alternative methods for recording information in academic settings, such as speech-to-text software or word processors and report a positive impact on her learning experience.

SMART Revision: By [end of term/specific date], Amelia will independently use at least two alternative recording methods (e.g., speech-to-text software and word processor) for 80% of written assignments requiring more than [X words/paragraphs]. Impact will be measured objectively by: (1) completion rate of written assignments increasing from [baseline %] to [target %], (2) word count increasing from average [X words] to [Y words] per assignment, (3) self-reported fatigue levels during writing tasks reducing from [baseline score] to [target score] on a 1-10 scale, and (4) work quality maintained or improved as evidenced by teacher assessment rubrics. Independence level will be tracked from initial adult support to independent initiation and use.

Note: This outcome is weak as: (1) "Report a positive impact" is subjective and poorly defined; (2) It lacks baseline data on current usage; (3) There is no timeframe or measurement of "utilise" (frequency, independence level); (4) The outcome doesn't specify how impact will be measured objectively. This should be redesigned to include: specific frequency of use (e.g., 80% of written assignments), measures of independence, objective metrics of impact such as completion rates, quality of work produced, or reduced fatigue levels, and clear timeframes.
