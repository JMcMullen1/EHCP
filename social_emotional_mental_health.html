NEED 4
Amelia's difficulties with school attendance are also impacted by her diagnosis of ASD and anxiety. Amelia is extremely concerned that someone will bump into her in school and cause her pain to increase. Within the school environment, Amelia displayed emotional shutdowns where she is unable to communicate with others and express her needs which leads her to being particularly vulnerable.
Outcomes
E5: Amelia will demonstrate the ability to express her emotions using an agreed strategy (e.g., verbally, or non-verbally) to trusted adults in 90% of situations where she experiences heightened emotional states, as observed by educational staff.

SMART Revision: By [end of term/specific date], Amelia will demonstrate a multi-level emotional communication system: (1) In early stages of emotional escalation (Level 1-2 on agreed 5-point scale), she will use agreed strategies (verbal labels, emotion cards, traffic light system) to express emotions to trusted adults in 70% of situations, improving from baseline of [X%]; (2) Recognize and use [3] early warning sign indicators to request support before reaching shutdown stage, tracked [daily/weekly]; (3) During emotional shutdowns when verbal communication is not possible, staff will implement agreed support protocols (use of visual communication aids, safe space access, minimal verbal demands) as evidenced by protocol adherence logs; (4) Recovery time from shutdown states will decrease from average baseline of [X minutes] to [Y minutes] over the review period. Success measured through incident logs, staff observations, and Amelia's post-event self-reports when able.

Note: This outcome partially addresses the communication aspect of emotional shutdowns but is weak in several ways: (1) It assumes Amelia will be able to communicate in 90% of situations, which may not be realistic given the severity of her shutdowns; (2) It does not address prevention of shutdowns or recovery strategies; (3) "Heightened emotional states" is vague and doesn't distinguish between different levels of distress; (4) It focuses only on expression to adults, not on supporting Amelia when she is unable to communicate. This should be redesigned to include: recognition of early warning signs of shutdowns, strategies to prevent escalation, alternative communication methods during shutdowns, and support protocols when verbal communication is not possible.
